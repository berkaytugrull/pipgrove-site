Health note

Pipgrove is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

If you have diabetes, are pregnant, take medication that affects blood sugar, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor before changing how you eat.

If food or eating feels out of your control, you deserve support. In the US you can reach the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline at 1-866-662-1235, or text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line.
