Privacy

On iPhone, Pipgrove keeps your data on your iPhone. Your check-ins, cravings, notes and settings are stored on the device and in a private iCloud copy linked to your Apple ID, so you can get your garden back on a new phone. We do not run a server that receives them, and there is no account.

The pocket coach runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. What you write to it is processed on the device and is not sent to us or saved.

Photos of your why stay on this phone and are not included in the iCloud copy.

The app downloads a small settings file from pipgrove.com, for example to know which offers to show. No personal data is sent with that request.

We do not sell or share personal data and we do not track you across apps.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, not your check-ins.

The barcode scanner looks products up in Open Food Facts, an open database. Only the barcode number is sent. Product data is © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the Open Database License.

To improve the app we count anonymous usage events with TelemetryDeck, such as "SOS finished" or "paywall viewed". They use a random install ID, never your name, email, notes, food or check-in contents, and cannot be used to identify you.

Buddy streak is optional. If you link with a friend, Pipgrove stores your first name, a random buddy code and the dates you checked in (not what you logged) in the app's public iCloud database, so your friend's app can show your shared streak. Stopping the buddy streak removes the link.

Pipgrove on Android

On Android, your check-ins, cravings, notes, photos of your why and settings are stored on the phone and, if you have it turned on, in Android's own device backup to your Google account, so you can get your garden back on a new phone. We do not run a server that receives them, and there is no account.

The Android app has no buddy streak, no iCloud copy and no pocket coach, so none of the data described for those features is collected on Android.

Purchases on Android are handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your Google Play purchase details, not your check-ins. The barcode scanner (Open Food Facts), the settings file and TelemetryDeck work the same way as described above. Pipgrove does not use the Android advertising ID and shows no ads.

Deleting your data

There is no Pipgrove account, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To delete everything the app stores, open Settings in the app and tap Delete all data (on iPhone this also removes the iCloud copy), or uninstall the app. Anonymous usage events in TelemetryDeck use a random install ID and cannot be linked to you. Subscriptions are not affected: cancel them in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play subscriptions. Questions: hello@pipgrove.com.

This website (pipgrove.com) uses Google Analytics to count visits, which pages are read and how many people tap through to the App Store. It sets analytics cookies, does not use advertising features, and is not connected to the app. You can opt out with a content blocker or at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

You can export or delete all of your data, including the iCloud copy, at any time in Settings.
