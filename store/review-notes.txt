2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Pipgrove is a gentle 28-day habit coach for adults who want to cut down on added sugar. The problem: sugar cravings are strong and most people quit diets after a slip. Pipgrove helps in the moment with a 2-minute craving SOS (notice, breathe, choose a swap), short daily lessons, and a garden that grows with every sugar-free day and every craving beaten. A slip never resets progress. It is not a medical app and does not give medical advice.

3. HOW TO USE / ACCESS FEATURES
No account or login is needed. Launch the app, answer the short onboarding questions, then on the plan screen either start the free trial or tap "Continue with the free version".
- Craving SOS: tap "Craving something sweet?" on Home (always free).
- Daily quests: Morning intention, 1-minute lesson, Evening check-in (opens in the evening) on Home.
- Tools tab: hidden-sugar barcode scanner and natural swap ideas.
- Progress tab: weekly pattern, badges, garden visitors.
- Journey tab: the 28-day garden stages.
- Pro: Settings (top-right profile icon) > Status "Free · see Pro" opens the paywall. Pro unlocks lessons and garden stages after the first week, unlimited scans (free: 3 a day), craving forecast, extra shields and full reports. Restore purchase is on the paywall.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple StoreKit via RevenueCat: subscriptions and restore.
- Apple CloudKit: optional "Buddy streak" (shares only a first name and check-in days with one invited friend).
- Open Food Facts public API: product sugar data for the barcode scanner.
- TelemetryDeck: anonymous, privacy-first usage counts (no personal data, no tracking).
No AI services, no login provider, no ads.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions; only prices are localized by the App Store.
