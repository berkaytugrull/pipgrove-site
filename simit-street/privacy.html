Simit Street – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Simit Street is a game made by Berkay Tuğrul ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses.

Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The iOS and Android versions show ads through Google AdMob. Most ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS we only use the advertising identifier if you allow tracking in the system prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA and UK you can choose your ad consent in the consent form shown in the game.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On web game portals (such as CrazyGames or Poki), ads and saves are handled by that portal under its own privacy policy.

Game analytics

To improve the game we send anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics (gameanalytics.com): for example which tutorial step was reached, day progress, session length, device model and OS version. They are linked to a random player ID created by the game, not to your name or advertising identifier, and are never sold. GameAnalytics privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Notifications

On iOS, if you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your till is full or your daily gift is ready). They are created locally on your phone, no notification data is sent to us, and you can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your progress in the game (Settings > Progress > Reset) or by deleting the app.
