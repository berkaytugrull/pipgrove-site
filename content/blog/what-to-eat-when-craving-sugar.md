When a sugar craving hits, "just eat an apple" can feel like a joke. The trick is to match the swap to what you actually want. Craving something creamy? An apple will not do it, but plain yogurt with berries might. Here are 20 options sorted by craving type.

## If you want something sweet

1. **Frozen grapes.** They taste like little sorbet bites.
2. **Berries** with a spoon of plain Greek yogurt.
3. **Two dates with a few almonds.** Sweet and chewy, with protein from the nuts.
4. **Apple slices with cinnamon** or peanut butter.
5. **Mango or pineapple** chunks, fresh or frozen.
6. **Baked pear or apple** with cinnamon, warm from the oven or microwave.

## If you want chocolate

7. **Dark chocolate, 70% or more,** one or two squares. Less sugar per bite and more flavour, so a little goes further.
8. **Cocoa with warm milk**, unsweetened or with a little cinnamon.
9. **Greek yogurt with cocoa powder** and a few raspberries.
10. **Banana "nice cream":** frozen banana blended with cocoa.

## If you want something creamy

11. **Plain Greek yogurt** with fruit and a few nuts.
12. **Cottage cheese with peaches** or berries.
13. **Chia pudding** made with milk and topped with fruit.
14. **A smoothie** of milk or yogurt, frozen berries and a spoon of nut butter.

## If you want something crunchy

15. **A handful of nuts** or trail mix without candy.
16. **Roasted chickpeas** with cinnamon.
17. **Popcorn**, plain or with a little salt.
18. **Rice cakes with nut butter** and banana slices.

## If you are actually hungry

19. **A boiled egg and fruit.**
20. **Hummus with carrots and cucumber.**

Ask yourself: would a plate of eggs sound good right now? If yes, you are hungry, so eat a real snack or meal. If only cake will do, it is a craving, and the [tips for stopping sugar cravings](/blog/how-to-stop-sugar-cravings/) help more than food.

## Why fruit works better than candy

Whole fruit has natural sugar, but it comes with fibre and water, which slow you down and fill you up. Fruit sugar is not counted toward the daily added sugar limits from the American Heart Association. Fruit juice and dried fruit are more concentrated, so keep those smaller.

## Make the good option the easy option

- Keep washed fruit at eye level in the fridge and on the counter.
- Pre-portion nuts into small bags.
- Freeze grapes and banana slices on the weekend.
- Keep tea within reach for after-dinner cravings.
