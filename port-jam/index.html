Port Jam: Boat Sort Puzzle – Support

Tap boats out of the jam, dock them at the piers and catch the barrels of their color as they swirl around the lighthouse.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Email hello@pipgrove.com. We usually reply within 2 days.

Purchases and subscription

To restore purchases (No Ads, Starter Pack, All Flags) on a new device, open the Shop in the game and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom.

Captain's Club is an optional auto-renewable subscription. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or on Android in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Friends

The Friends screen uses a random captain name and a 6-character friend code. You can delete your friend data at any time from the Friends screen. Without the app, see how to request deletion.
