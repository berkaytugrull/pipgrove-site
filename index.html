Questions

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@pipgrove.com and a real person answers.

Do I have to quit sugar completely?

No. You can go sugar-free for 28 days or cut down step by step. If you cut down, less-sugar days count too.

What happens if I slip?

Your log shows it honestly, but your sugar-free days and your garden are never lost. Pip asks what led to it and helps you plan for next time. Every seven clean days also earns a shield that keeps your garden growing on a hard day.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. There is no account and we do not sell or share personal data. Read the privacy policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pipgrove is a habit coach. If you have diabetes, an eating disorder or another health condition, talk to your doctor. See our health note and support lines.

Can I do it with a friend?

Yes. Invite a buddy and keep a shared streak. Only your first name and check-in dates are shared, never what you logged.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Android is not available yet.
