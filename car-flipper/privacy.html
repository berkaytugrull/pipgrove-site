Car Flipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Car Flipper (Galerici in Turkish) is a single-player game made by Berkay Tuğrul ("we"), published on Google Play as Moontoo Studio (BERKAY TUĞRUL BİLİŞİM). It is available for iPhone, Android and on web game portals. This policy explains what data the game uses.

Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The iOS and Android versions show ads through Google AdMob. Most ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch; a short interstitial may appear at the end of an in-game day after the first days of play. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS we only use the advertising identifier if you allow tracking in the system prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA and UK you can choose your ad consent in the consent form shown in the game.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).

On web game portals (such as CrazyGames or GameDistribution), ads and saves are handled by that portal under its own privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details. The game asks the store which products you own so it can deliver and restore them.

Game analytics

To improve the game we send anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics (gameanalytics.com): for example which tutorial step was reached, day progress, sales results, session length, device model and OS version. They are linked to a random player ID created by the game, not to your name or advertising identifier, and are never sold. GameAnalytics privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when a repair is finished or tomorrow's deal is ready). They are created locally on your phone, no notification data is sent to us, and you can turn them off in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your data

We do not keep any account or player data on our own servers. You can delete your progress by deleting the app (or clearing its storage). Anonymous analytics data is kept by GameAnalytics under its own retention policy; to ask for its deletion, email hello@pipgrove.com with the subject "Car Flipper data deletion" and we will reply within 30 days.
