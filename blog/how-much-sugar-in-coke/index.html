How Much Sugar Is in a Coke? Shown in Sugar Cubes

A 12 oz can of Coca-Cola has 39 g of sugar, about 10 sugar cubes. See how Coke compares with Pepsi, Sprite, Mountain Dew and Red Bull.

By the Pipgrove team. November 11, 2026, 2 min read.

A 12 oz (355 ml) can of Coca-Cola has 39 grams of sugar. That is about 10 sugar cubes, or a little over 9 teaspoons, all of it added sugar. One can is already more than the 25 g a day the American Heart Association suggests for most women, and most of the 36 g it suggests for men (AHA).

See it in sugar cubes

Pick a drink. Each cube is about 4 g of sugar. Red cubes go past a 25 g daily limit.

Coke sizes compared

Values are from US Coca-Cola labels and can change by country and recipe. Many countries outside the US now sell lower-sugar versions, so check the label where you live.

Why a drink hits differently from food

Sugar in a drink goes down fast and does not fill you up the way food does. You can finish 39 g in a few minutes and still feel just as hungry at your next meal. That is why sweet drinks are usually the easiest and biggest win when you cut back.

Easy swaps that still feel like a treat

  • Sparkling water with lime, lemon or a splash of juice.
  • Coke Zero Sugar or Diet Coke as a bridge. They have no sugar, and many people use them for a few weeks while their taste for sweetness resets.
  • Unsweetened iced tea with mint or lemon.
  • Half and half. If you cannot quit cold, mix half soda and half sparkling water for a week, then go further.

Quick answers

How many sugar cubes are in a can of Coke?

A 12 oz can of Coca-Cola has 39 g of sugar. At about 4 g per cube, that is roughly 10 sugar cubes.

How much sugar is in a 20 oz Coke?

A 20 oz bottle of Coca-Cola has 65 g of sugar, about 16 sugar cubes. That is more than double the 25 g daily limit the American Heart Association suggests for most women.

Does Coke Zero have sugar?

No. Coke Zero Sugar and Diet Coke have 0 g of sugar. They are sweetened with low-calorie sweeteners instead.

Is the sugar in Coke added sugar?

Yes. All of the sugar in regular Coca-Cola is added sugar, so it counts fully toward your daily added sugar limit.
